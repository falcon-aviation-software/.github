# Falcon Aviation Software

We build **[FalconMX](https://falconmx.app)**, a maintenance-first platform for aviation maintenance shops.

Technicians run their work by talking to FalconMX in plain language, from the office or the hangar floor, instead of filling in forms. FalconMX keeps an aircraft's maintenance records digital and portable, and handles the paperwork around the work itself.

## What FalconMX does

- **Work orders by voice or text.** Open a work order, log time, write up discrepancies and close it out by describing the work in your own words.
- **Digital logbooks.** Completed work becomes a logbook entry, stored digitally and printed as a label for the paper logbook. Existing paper logbooks can be imported into structured records.
- **AD compliance research.** FAA Airworthiness Directives are pulled in daily. FalconMX checks an aircraft's records against them, and a certificated technician approves every result.
- **Estimates and invoices.** Aircraft owners accept or decline estimate lines from a link, and the accepted work becomes invoices for your accounting software.
- **Records that follow the aircraft.** Aircraft records belong to the aircraft, not to one shop, so its history goes with it from shop to shop.
